About

I'm a Cyber Security student in Sheridan College's Honours Bachelor of Information Sciences program, focused on where application security meets AI.

As an AI researcher at Sheridan I evaluate how large language models behave under different prompts and framing, and I build tooling to test whether AI coding agents can be pushed into writing insecure code. Outside of research I compete in CTFs and build security-first web apps.
